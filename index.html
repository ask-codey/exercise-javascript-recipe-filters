  • Overnight Oats

    5 min · breakfast

    1. Stir oats, milk and a spoonful of honey together in a jar.
    2. Close the jar and leave it in the fridge overnight.
    3. Top with berries in the morning.

  • Sheet-Pan Fajitas

    30 min · dinner

    1. Slice two peppers, an onion and two chicken breasts.
    2. Toss them with oil and fajita spice on a sheet pan.
    3. Roast at 425°F for 20 minutes.
    4. Fill warm tortillas and add salsa.
